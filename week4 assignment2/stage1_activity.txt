Task 2 – Identify current problems

1. Paper records can be lost, damaged or difficult to find.
2. Spreadsheets can contain errors or duplicate information.
3. Managing appointments manually can cause scheduling conflicts and missed appointments.
4. It may be difficult for staff to quickly access and update accurate patient information.

Task 3 – Ask client questions

The purpose here is to show that the original client statement is missing important requirements.

1. Who will use the new system?
2. What information about patients needs to be stored?
3. How are appointments currently booked, changed and cancelled?
4. Who should be allowed to access patient records?
5. Does the clinic need the system to integrate with any existing software or systems?

Why shouldn’t we automatically trust AI?

AI can generate useful ideas, but it doesn’t automatically know the client’s real requirements. It can make assumptions, misunderstand the problem, suggest unnecessary features, or produce unsafe recommendations. A software engineer must verify AI output against requirements, stakeholders, security, legal obligations and risks.

Exit Question

Write one activity that a software engineer must perform and that cannot safely be delegated entirely to AI.

Understanding and validating the client’s requirements with stakeholders.

A software engineer must talk to the client and users, clarify what they actually need, identify risks and make sure the requirements are correct. AI can help suggest questions or ideas, but it should not make these decisions independently.
